Built for Canadian Personal Finance

Canadian personal finance is genuinely different. TFSAs, RRSPs, CPP, OAS, CMHC, the mortgage stress test, HST, land transfer tax — these are Canadian realities that generic calculators from the US ignore or get wrong. Every tool on Northern Numbers is built specifically for Canada, uses current 2026 rates, and is completely free.

Always Free. Always Accurate.

Northern Numbers is free to use with no account, no paywall, and no locked features. Every calculator is built from primary sources — CRA tables, Statistics Canada data, OSFI guidelines, and provincial government schedules — and updated when rates change. We are based in Ontario, Canada.
