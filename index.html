Built for Canadian Personal Finance

Canadian personal finance is genuinely different. TFSAs, RRSPs, CPP, OAS, CMHC, the mortgage stress test, HST, land transfer tax — these are Canadian realities that generic calculators from the US ignore or get wrong. Every tool on Northern Numbers is built specifically for Canada, uses current 2026 rates, and is completely free.

🏦

Registered Accounts

TFSA growth projections, TFSA contribution room by birth year, RRSP growth and tax refund estimates, FHSA first-home savings, RESP with Canada Education Savings Grant, and RRIF minimum withdrawal schedules using official CRA prescribed factors.

🏠

Real Estate & Mortgages

Mortgage payments with Canadian semi-annual compounding, mortgage affordability using the OSFI stress test and GDS/TDS ratios, land transfer tax for all 13 provinces including Toronto municipal tax and first-time buyer rebates, and a rent vs buy comparison.

🧾

Tax Calculators

Federal and provincial income tax for all 13 provinces and territories using 2026 brackets, take-home paycheck calculator with CPP, CPP2, and EI, GST/HST calculator for all provinces, and an inflation calculator using Canadian CPI data.

🇨🇦

Retirement Planning

CPP benefit estimates at age 60, 65, and 70 with deferral bonuses, OAS pension with GIS eligibility and clawback thresholds, and RRIF minimum withdrawals by age with year-by-year balance projections — all using 2026 CRA rules.

Always Free. Always Accurate.

Northern Numbers is free to use with no account, no paywall, and no locked features. Every calculator is built from primary sources — CRA tables, Statistics Canada data, OSFI guidelines, and provincial government schedules — and updated when rates change. We are based in Ontario, Canada.

Results are estimates for planning purposes. Always verify important figures with your financial institution, CRA My Account, or a qualified advisor before making major financial decisions. See our full disclaimer.
