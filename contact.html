What We Can Help With

🔢

Reporting a Calculation Error

If you believe a rate, formula, or result is incorrect, please email us with the calculator name, the inputs you used, the result you got, and what you expected to see. We take accuracy seriously and investigate every report. If you can link to the official CRA or government source showing the correct figure, that helps a lot.

💡

Suggesting a New Calculator

We're always adding new calculators. If there's a Canadian financial calculation you need that we don't have yet — a specific tax credit, a provincial benefit, a pension estimate, or anything else — let us know. We prioritize requests that are genuinely Canada-specific and hard to find elsewhere.

❓

General Questions

If you have a question about how a calculator works, what a specific input means, or how a Canadian financial rule applies to your situation, feel free to ask. We can explain the methodology behind our calculators. Please note that we are not licensed financial advisors and cannot provide personalized financial advice.

🤝

Business and Partnership Inquiries

For media mentions, partnerships, or other business inquiries, email us at hello@northernnumbers.ca with a brief description of what you have in mind.

What We Can't Help With

Northern Numbers is a calculator tool, not a financial advisory service. We cannot provide personalized tax advice, investment recommendations, legal opinions, or help interpreting your specific CRA notices. For those needs, please consult a licensed tax professional, financial advisor, or the CRA directly at 1-800-959-8281.

Response Times

We aim to respond to all emails within 1–3 business days. During periods of high volume (particularly around tax season, January TFSA room updates, and federal budget announcements), response times may be slightly longer. Calculation error reports are always prioritized.
